Why We Make It This Way

We'd rather sell you one coat than four jackets

Sapphire started as a rejection of the six-week drop cycle. We hold a standing collection instead of seasons — the same eleven fabrics, refined a little each year, cut in a workshop outside Lisbon that we've worked with since day one.

Every garment carries a code instead of a name. Ask us for SPH-CT-002 in five years and we'll still know exactly what thread it's cut from.
