The honest playbook

Four levers decide every revenue curve. Large outcomes come from compounding all four.

Acquire cheaply

Growth in new customers is the steepest lever. Keep CAC well below lifetime value (LTV/CAC above 3).

Keep them

Churn quietly caps your ceiling. At 5% monthly churn, steady state is just 20× your monthly new customers.

Expand revenue

Raising revenue per customer over time compounds without needing a single extra user.

Grow the market

Every model has a ceiling set by addressable market. Past it, only new products or new markets move the line.
